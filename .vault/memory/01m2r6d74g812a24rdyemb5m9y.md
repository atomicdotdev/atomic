On this machine /tmp is a 15G tmpfs with a hard quota; cargo builds that exceed it fail mid-compile with 'Disk quota exceeded' while writing cc temp files (e.g. libsqlite3-sys), even when df still shows free space. Long-running builds in /tmp/atomic must use CARGO_TARGET_DIR under /home (e.g. ~/.cache/atomic-target, 262G free on the root fs) and prune old /tmp work dirs.
